## Build note — a hang that was NOT a toolchain defect

Stage 1 hung twice: vite at 0.0 % CPU with an empty `dist/` for 5 and 10
minutes. The obvious reading was the Node 25 incompatibility that has broken
vitest all session. It was not. The first `pkill` left processes behind and the
retry contended with them; after a full cleanup vite built in **29 s**. Worth
recording because the wrong diagnosis was one step away and would have been
reported as a blocking toolchain problem.
